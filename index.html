Our story

Beautiful glassbox.
Without any effort.

Aquariums are some of the most stunningly beautiful objects we can bring indoors. However, they require good grasp of aquarium knowledge and experience, and constant effort and attention.

Our company, Jintae Aquarium, aims to achieve aquariums that require no maintenance at all. We will develop technological systems to do so.
